CHIM Cached Connector v1.3.4 - Release Notes
============================================
Release Date: 2026-01-16
Compatible with: CHIM 2.0.3

OVERVIEW
--------
v1.3.4 fixes an issue where asterisks, dashes, and other punctuation were
being stripped from the start of sentences when the LLM didn't include a
space after the sentence-ending period.

BUG FIX: ASTERISK/DASH STRIPPING
---------------------------------
This release fixes a regression where action/narration markers were being
consumed by the sentence splitting regex.

CHANGES FROM v1.3.3
-------------------

1. FIXED PUNCTUATION STRIPPING IN SENTENCE SPLITTING

   **Problem:**
   Asterisks and dashes at the start of sentences were being stripped when
   there was no space between the period and the punctuation:

   - Input: `Hello there.* rises from chair *.`
   - Before: Sentence 2 was `rises from chair *.` (asterisk missing!)
   - After: Sentence 2 is `* rises from chair *.` (asterisk preserved)

   **Why It Was Inconsistent:**
   The issue only occurred when the LLM omitted the space after the period:
   - `Hello. * rises...` → asterisk preserved (space before asterisk)
   - `Hello.* rises...` → asterisk consumed (no space)

   **Root Cause:**
   The regex `[\p{P}]?` in `split_at_end_of_sentence()` was consuming ANY
   Unicode punctuation character immediately after sentence-ending marks.

   **Solution:**
   Changed from consuming all punctuation to explicitly excluding problematic
   characters while still consuming closing quotes and brackets:

   Old: `[\p{P}]?` (any punctuation)
   New: `[^\p{L}\p{N}\s\*\-\#\@\(\[\{]?` (exclude letters, numbers, whitespace,
        asterisks, dashes, hashes, at-signs, opening brackets)

2. CHARACTERS NOW PRESERVED

   The following are no longer stripped from sentence starts:
   - `*` - Asterisks (action/narration markers)
   - `-` - Dashes (pause indicators)
   - `#` - Hashes (tags)
   - `@` - At signs (mentions)
   - `(` `[` `{` - Opening brackets

3. CHARACTERS STILL CONSUMED (CORRECT BEHAVIOR)

   These are still consumed after sentence-ending punctuation:
   - `"` `'` - Closing quotes
   - `)` `]` `}` - Closing brackets
   - Combined punctuation like `?!`
